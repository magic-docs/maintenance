# Business Plan (Israel) — Solo Computer Maintenance & Refurbishment Service

## 1) Executive Summary
This business provides on-site and remote IT maintenance for schools and small businesses in Israel, with a strong focus on extending hardware life, reducing IT costs, and improving system reliability.

Core value proposition:
- Make existing computers faster, safer, and more stable.
- Refurbish aging devices (SSD/RAM upgrades, thermal service, OS refresh).
- Migrate very old hardware to lightweight Linux distributions when Windows performance is no longer viable.
- Set up practical identity/domain and backup foundations for small organizations.

Business structure at launch:
- **One-person owner-operator** (technical + sales + operations).
- Service area: local city + nearby region (expand gradually based on contracts).
- Revenue model: one-off projects + recurring maintenance plans.

---

## 2) Target Market (Israel)

### Primary segments
1. **Schools and educational centers**
   - Computer labs with mixed-age hardware.
   - Need predictable uptime and low total cost.
2. **Small businesses (5–75 endpoints)**
   - Offices without full-time IT staff.
   - Need practical, responsive support and preventive maintenance.
3. **Non-profits and municipal/community centers**
   - Cost-sensitive environments where hardware extension has high ROI.

### Typical pains solved
- Slow PCs and frequent crashes.
- Aging devices that are “almost unusable”.
- Weak patching/backup discipline.
- No clear inventory or lifecycle tracking.
- Too much dependence on ad-hoc break/fix support.

---

## 3) Services

### A. Maintenance & Optimization
- Deep cleaning (dust/fan/heatsink).
- Performance optimization (startup/services/storage health).
- Malware/adware checks.
- Updates, driver review, health diagnostics.

### B. Refurbishment & Upgrade
- SSD and RAM upgrades.
- Thermal paste replacement (where needed).
- OS refresh/reinstallation.
- Device viability classification: keep / upgrade / replace.

### C. Legacy Hardware Rescue (Linux Path)
- Install and configure Ubuntu LTS / Linux Mint XFCE / Lubuntu / AlmaLinux (based on use case).
- Set up browser, office suite, printing, Hebrew locale.
- User orientation and transition support.

### D. Small-Org IT Foundation
- Domain/account architecture (cloud or local directory approach based on client constraints).
- Shared folders and permission model.
- Endpoint update policy.
- Backup setup + restore validation.

### E. Managed Maintenance Plans
- Monthly/quarterly proactive checks.
- Remote support windows.
- Patch compliance tracking.
- Asset status reporting.

## 4) Pricing Strategy (Israel, 2026 positioning)
Pricing is set to be competitive for solo professional service while preserving sustainable margins.

### Baseline service rates (before VAT)
- Remote support: **₪170/hour**
- On-site standard support: **₪220/hour**
- Urgent / same-day premium: **₪300/hour**
- Minimum on-site charge: **₪450 per visit**

### Common fixed-price items (before VAT)
- Workstation cleanup + optimization: **₪220 per PC**
- Deep hardware clean + thermal service: **₪280 per PC**
- SSD migration labor (excluding parts): **₪300 per PC**
- Linux migration package (single user workstation): **₪550 per PC**
- Initial network/domain assessment (small site): **₪1,200**

### Managed plans (before VAT)
- **Basic Plan (up to 20 devices):** ₪1,800/month  
- **Standard Plan (20–50 devices):** ₪3,600/month  
- **Plus Plan (50–80 devices):** ₪6,200/month  

Assumption: plans include defined remote support quotas and scheduled preventive windows; extra on-site work billed at contracted discounted rates.

## 5) Delivery Model for a One-Person Operation

### Capacity constraints and rules
- Focus on **1–2 anchor clients** first (recurring contracts), then expand.
- Batch on-site days by geography to reduce travel overhead.
- Keep emergency slots blocked each week.
- Use standardized checklists and templates to maintain quality and speed.

### Suggested weekly operating rhythm
- 2 days: on-site maintenance/projects
- 2 days: remote support + documentation + sales follow-up
- 1 day: overflow / urgent calls / procurement

### SLA positioning (realistic for solo)
- Remote first response: within 4 business hours
- On-site standard response: next business day
- Urgent response: same day when slot available (premium rate)

## 8) Financial Model (Solo, conservative)

### Startup investment (tools + initial stock)
Expected range from equipment file: **~₪7,000 to ₪13,000** depending on initial spare-part inventory depth.

### Monthly fixed costs (example envelope)
- Transport/fuel: ₪1,200–₪2,000
- Insurance/professional/admin/software: ₪1,000–₪2,000
- Phone/internet/misc: ₪400–₪800
- Total fixed envelope: **₪2,600–₪4,800**

### Revenue targets
- Month 1–3 goal: ₪12,000–₪22,000 monthly revenue
- Month 4–9 goal: ₪25,000–₪40,000 monthly revenue
- Year 1 target: establish stable recurring base of ₪12,000+/month from contracts

### Break-even intuition
At an average blended billable rate near ₪210/hour equivalent, break-even is typically reached with ~15–25 billable hours/month after fixed costs, depending on travel and tooling amortization.

---

## 9) Risks and Mitigations

1. **Single-person bandwidth risk**
   - Mitigation: strict intake limits, emergency pricing, standardized workflows.
2. **Cashflow variability**
   - Mitigation: increase share of monthly retainers.
3. **Scope creep**
   - Mitigation: signed scope and change-order policy.
4. **Hardware uncertainty on old machines**
   - Mitigation: explicit no-guarantee language for end-of-life devices, with documented risk class.

## 10) 12-Month Milestones
- **Q1:** 3–5 active clients, 1 recurring school/small business contract.
- **Q2:** 2 recurring contracts + repeat project pipeline.
- **Q3:** Formalized preventive maintenance cadence across clients.
- **Q4:** Decide whether to remain solo or add part-time field help.
